Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pang Teojyo (“the App”) handles information when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

The App stores game scores and a gameplay counter on your device. The counter is used to determine when to show an advertisement after a certain number of games. This information is stored locally using your device’s app storage. The App does not send your scores or counter to the developer.

Advertisements

The App may show an advertisement during a break after you have played a certain number of games. Advertisements may be provided by a third-party advertising service. If ads are served, that provider may collect or receive information such as your device or advertising identifier, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, or comply with legal requirements.

The advertising provider’s collection and use of information are governed by its own privacy policy. The App’s policy and disclosures should be updated with the provider’s name and privacy-policy link when an advertising service is selected and integrated.

Network connection

The App checks your device’s network connection to determine whether online content, including advertisements, can be shown. The connection check itself does not send your scores or gameplay counter to the developer.

Information collected by the developer

The App does not require an account. The developer does not receive game scores or the gameplay counter through the App. Your device’s operating system, app store, or any advertising provider may process information under its own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children directly. If advertisements are shown, the advertising service may process information as described in its privacy policy and in accordance with applicable requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game records and the gameplay counter remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. The developer does not receive or retain this locally stored information. Any information handled by an advertising provider is subject to that provider’s retention practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be made available with a revised effective date.
